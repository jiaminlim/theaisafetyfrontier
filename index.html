Anthropic's Claude produced the first complete computer-checked proof of Fermat's Last Theorem, writing 13 million lines of Lean across roughly 30,300 theorems over 11 days of largely autonomous work — a formalization of Andrew Wiles's 1995 proof that Imperial College London mathematician Kevin Buzzard verified rests on no assumptions beyond the axioms of mathematics.
